Asymmetry Tracker: Mapping Structural Power and Legal Double Standards

The Asymmetry Tracker is an independent, open-source monitoring index dedicated to documenting the material, legal, and institutional power imbalances in occupied Palestine. Rather than framing the situation as a bilateral diplomatic dispute between two equal states, this tracker provides a fact-based archive of structural asymmetry, mapping how state legislation and corporate technology contracts actively maintain a system of displacement.

1. Legal Asymmetry: The Institutionalized Double Standard of Return

The core demographic architecture of the region is governed by a profound legal contradiction that privileges one global group while systematically denying the fundamental human rights of the indigenous population.

The Law of Return (1950) vs. Palestinian Right of Return

Passed by the Israeli Knesset, this state law grants any individual worldwide who converts to Judaism—regardless of their biological ancestry or geographic origin—an automatic right to migrate to Israel and claim full citizenship, state-subsidized housing, and immediate political rights.

Concurrently, millions of indigenous Palestinians hold documented familial land deeds, keys, and direct lineage to specific villages from which they were violently expelled during the 1948 Nakba and the 1967 war. Under state emergency regulations and residency laws, these individuals are legally barred from returning to their homes, remaining in the territory, or claiming self-determination.

The Structural Reality: A systemic legal framework where a foreign convert holds superior territorial and political rights over a biologically continuous, indigenous inhabitant who was physically displaced by state force.

2. Technological Asymmetry: The Political Economy of Project Nimbus

Systemic oppression in the digital era relies heavily on the complicity of multinational technology conglomerates. The primary infrastructure enabling automated state surveillance and territorial control is Project Nimbus.

What is Project Nimbus?

Project Nimbus is a $1.2 billion cloud computing and artificial intelligence contract signed in 2021 between the Israeli government/military and two Western tech giants: Google and Amazon.

The Operational Impact

The contract provides the Israeli military and defense establishment with enterprise-level cloud infrastructure, data centers, and advanced machine-learning capabilities. Human rights organizations and internal tech whistleblowers document that this infrastructure directly enables automated facial recognition databases used at military checkpoints in the West Bank, data processing required to maintain the physical blockade of the Gaza Strip, and advanced algorithmic surveillance used to monitor Palestinian digital communications and movement.

The Contractual Safeguards against Protest

The Project Nimbus contract contains a strict, non-negotiable clause explicitly forbidding Google and Amazon from shutting down services or denying access to specific military branches due to international boycott campaigns, employee protests, or international legal sanctions. When Google employees organized internal sit-ins and protests against this military complicity, the corporation responded by firing dozens of workers, proving that corporate tech ownership actively prioritizes state defense contracts over ethical human rights baselines.

Conclusion

The data compiled by this tracker demonstrates that the status quo is maintained through a combination of discriminatory state legislation and multi-billion dollar corporate technology infrastructure. True objectivity requires analyzing these material assets and legal monopolies, rather than hiding behind sanitized diplomatic euphemisms.
